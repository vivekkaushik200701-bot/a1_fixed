Stargazing

Category: Nature

Time: 1 hour

Budget: Free

Why Try It?

Stargazing is a peaceful way to enjoy nature and discover the night sky. Find a safe spot away from bright lights and look for stars and constellations.

What You Need

  • A clear night sky
  • A safe outdoor place
  • A blanket or comfortable chair
  • About 1 hour of free time
