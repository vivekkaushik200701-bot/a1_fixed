Journaling

Category: Creativity

Time: 30 minutes

Budget: Free

Why Try It?

Journaling is a simple way to express your thoughts and remember your day. Write about your experiences, ideas or things you are grateful for.

What You Need

  • A notebook or journal
  • A pen or pencil
  • A quiet and comfortable place
  • About 30 minutes of free time
