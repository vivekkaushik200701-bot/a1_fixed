Picnic

Category: Relaxation

Time: 2 hours

Budget: ₹100

Why Try It?

A picnic is a relaxing way to enjoy a meal outdoors, get some fresh air and spend quality time with friends or family.

What You Need

  • A blanket to sit on
  • Snacks or a packed meal
  • A nearby park or outdoor spot
  • About 2 hours of free time
